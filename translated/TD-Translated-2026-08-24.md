## Title: On August 24th, a day that seems like nothing special.
## Date: 2026-08-24
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Recently, I wrote a rather definitive line: "When you feel the first signs of autumn, that's summer fatigue setting in."

I've felt this way since about two years ago. When you notice things like "the wind has turned cooler" or "the cicadas are singing differently," that means autumn is on its way, right? I think I've got that part correct. And it's right around when you feel those things that "your body starts acting strange."

In the dead of summer, when I was constantly going on about how hot it was, I could blame everything on the heat, so I never noticed. But all those days of it pile up as fatigue in the body. And then, once things cool down even a little, that's when you realize, "Ah, I'm exhausted." That's how I've come to think of it.

You know how when you're out running you don't sweat while you're moving, but the moment you stop, you're suddenly drenched? I feel like it's something like that.

But I have no idea whether any of this is actually true. It's really nothing more than my own personal "explanation of summer fatigue." For that matter, I don't even really understand the concept of "summer fatigue" itself. Do people get worn out at the end of summer, in every country? Until a few years ago, I didn't even know what "summer fatigue" was.

(In moments like this, if you just ask an AI, the answer comes right up. Let's see now, oh, I see, ah, that does make sense...)

...Summer fatigue is a real thing. And it seems my own sense of it wasn't far off, either. In summer, the body works hard to cope with the heat. Regulating body temperature, the strain on the autonomic nervous system, fluid balance, lack of sleep, all of it, the body wears itself out adapting to the heat. And then, just as you've finally gotten a body suited to the heat, the "autumn wind" starts to blow. Of course you end up thinking, "Oh, come on, really?" Absolutely.

So it comes back to that opening line, "When you feel the first signs of autumn, that's summer fatigue setting in." That's it. And I've definitely got summer fatigue.
