## Title: I want to make life easier for everyone.
## Date: 2026-08-13
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Even without deliberately deciding to live, we go on living quite naturally, don't we. And along the way, we naturally do things that make life "easier to live." Eating, for instance, makes life easier to live for us. If we didn't eat, life would become harder to live. The same goes for sleeping, or relieving ourselves. These are all important things we do to make ourselves "easier to live." And building an environment where we can reliably do such things, that too is a way of making life "easier to live."

Some people might think there's a bare minimum of this and that which is all you need in order to live. But in reality, even when the bare minimum is in place, life doesn't become "easier to live." Simply having the conditions met means you can "live," yes, but it doesn't make life any easier to live. After all, someone serving a sentence in a prison facility has their "living" guaranteed too, don't they. And yet, that alone is precisely what makes it a "punishment."

Being able to use convenient things makes life easier to live. Having money makes life easier to live. Hurting others makes life harder to live. Deceiving others makes life harder to live. Being kind to others makes life easier to live.

What is it that makes life easier to live, and what makes it harder to live? Sometimes something makes life harder to live for a while, and yet eventually it's the very thing that makes life easier to live. Perhaps all of us, day by day, are living our lives working our hardest to make them "easier to live."
