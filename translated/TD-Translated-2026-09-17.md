## Title: Is there motivation, or was there ever any?
## Date: 2026-09-17
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Yesterday I wrote, "What on earth is mental strength, anyway?" I simply put down, as it was, the fact that I don't really understand it. But I did notice one thing: that "mental strength" is less something you seek for yourself and more something others demand of you, the fans, the supporters and all the rest.

Today I want to write a little about "desire." It's probably a much bigger theme than I can handle, but I thought I'd write about the word "desire" that has stuck with me ever since childhood.

Once, within earshot of me, some grown-ups were talking. "Once this kid," meaning me, "develops some desire, he'll really grow." In other words, I was regarded as someone who had no "desire." They spoke about it hopefully, as in, "It'll come to him eventually."

What was that "desire" they meant? As for whether any "desire" ever emerged in me after I overheard that, well, it seems it never did.

What the grown-ups were calling "desire" in that moment was, I think now, closer to "drive," or the will to strive.

To be honest, when I sense that kind of drive in other people, I think, "How wonderful." And yet I can hardly ever find that drive in myself. It's not that I lack energy. I'm perfectly lively. It's just that I have no real sense of possessing any "drive." Do I ever put on a performance of "drive"? Maybe a little. Actually, not even that much. Perhaps just a touch.

The boy who, back in elementary school, was supposed to grow stronger "once desire came to him," is he still, even now, unable to summon it? Perhaps, just like "mental strength," "desire," or drive, isn't something that wells up from within you after all.

No, I do want to eat things, I do want to do things. But that feels like something different from "drive," somehow.
