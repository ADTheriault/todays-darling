## Title: This time, the congratulations come from me
## Date: 2026-11-10
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Once a certain amount of time has gone by, people often say, "It went by in a flash." They say things like, "It felt long, but really it was short." Today I turned seventy-eight, and let me tell you, it did not go by in a flash. Not even close. I don't for a moment think it felt long but turned out short. It's long. Seventy-eight years, in its own way, is not short at all. I want the eighteen-year-olds to know this, and the sixty-eight-year-olds too.

Every single day, you wake up and you go to sleep, you work and you play, you laugh, you grieve, you get angry, you get bored, and you do all of it, day after day. I don't remember being born, but I remember a surprising amount from when I was very small. And after that, as a boy, as a young man, and once I'd become a working adult of sorts, I've held on to it, piece by piece. And yet what I've forgotten is far, far greater.

There hasn't been a single day I spent out cold, so if you count them up one at a time, that makes 28,489 days I've been alive. Try counting on your fingers, one, two, three, four, five. Even getting to a thousand is hard work. That's folding a finger down a thousand times. It's a lot. Nobody would even try. And yet living, day by day, is something we do every day, and we keep it up for tens of thousands of days. 28,489 of them, say. It's not as if I've lived each of those days with all my might. Still, I think it's really quite something.

It feels like I've seen a lot of people off, and I've met many more. Far from going by in a flash, it's been more long days than I could ever remember. And today, one more day gets added to the pile. Pretty amazing, really.
