## Title: Just write today, no matter what, a little at a time.
## Date: 2026-09-19
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I got into an elevator with my daughter, and with her daughter too, meaning my granddaughter. At the back of the elevator, straight ahead, there was a mirror. My seven-year-old granddaughter pulled a funny face at it. My daughter watched and laughed along with her. But I wasn't in the mirror at all. Where on earth was I?

They call it Silver Week, but did such a thing ever exist in the old days? I knew about Golden Week, but did they set this one up in autumn just to rival it? There was already the "Respect for the Aged Day," so maybe they lumped everything around there together under the banner of "silver"? I won't look it up. I've fallen into this habit of checking every little thing, and knowing the "supposedly correct information" about everything has stopped being much fun. Maybe I'll just decide it doesn't exist at all. Silver Week is a lie. There's no such thing.

Language is a convenient thing. Give it a subject and a predicate, and anything at all can be made to stand. He is fleet of foot. A little while ago he ran at three thousand kilometers an hour. The sneakers he was wearing at the time are right here. The soles have worn down considerably from the friction heat. I'll hand these sneakers over to anyone who wants them. I bought them for two hundred yen, so fifty thousand will do. Whether it's true or not, as long as it's grammatically sound, you can spin the whole tale as if it were gospel.

Every day, I've made a habit of "copying Chiikawa" as calligraphy practice. How did the lines that were drawn come to be? When you practice by copying, you naturally start to imagine it. Perhaps, even more than the act of drawing with a brush in hand, imagining what happened during the time the thing was drawn is the more important part.

Thank you for coming to Hobonichi again today. It's perfectly fine, you know, to write a little freely, like dancing however you please.
