## Title: Why? Why ask why?
## Date: 2026-10-14
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Our dog isn't especially clingy. I know plenty of dogs express their joy with their entire bodies, all "I love you, I love you, wahoo, yippee!", but watching ours makes it very clear that not every dog is like that.

Our previous dog was also the type who liked walks more than people, yet every night she slept at the foot of my bed. The dog we have now first politely takes care of her little business, then hops up beside the pillow on Mom's bed and sleeps there for a while, her rump pressed up against her. Before long, though, she drops lightly off the bed and settles into her own, where she can sleep alone in peace. She never asks to be petted, but she doesn't refuse it while it's happening, either. Really, the way she relates to us is cool, or maybe just room temperature.

And yet this same dog, when the mood strikes her while she's with me, will suddenly start licking my palm. With an expression so serious it borders on ferocious, she pins my hand down with her forepaws and licks the entire palm, hard, on and on and on. When she's finished with my left hand, she goes looking for my right and starts all over again. She doesn't stop.

I tried to think of this as a sign of affection. But it's so intense, so rough, so relentless, that "affection" simply doesn't fit. Nor does it seem to be a thank-you for everything I do for her. Besides, I'm not exactly confident about the taste or smell of my palms. Half the time I've only just washed my hands.

I looked into it in all sorts of ways but couldn't find an answer. I asked AI twice, at different times, and got nowhere. So in the end I arrived at this conclusion: just as dogs gnaw tirelessly and with total devotion on horse tendons, chunks of wood, or stuffed toys, she licks my hand for no reason at all except that she wants to lick my hand. If only I hadn't gone and asked "WHY?", it was probably something I'd understood from the very start.

Today's question, no. 071: "What's something you do without knowing why?" A quirk? A habit? If you think of one, write it in an email and send it my way.
