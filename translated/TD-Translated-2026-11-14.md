## Title: On being someone who can't be bothered
## Date: 2026-11-14
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I never had any particular philosophy about raising children, but as my daughter was growing up, I did hope she would become a child who didn't say "what a bother." Someone who wouldn't complain that things were a hassle, who wouldn't spare herself the effort. When we took the subway together, I'd ride the escalator while she climbed the stairs beside it. Somehow that just carried on, year after year, as if it were the natural order of things. Whether it's been passed down to her own daughter, I couldn't tell you.

Why did I think this way? Because I was exactly the sort of person who found everything a bother and wanted to spare himself any effort. When you have a weakness, you end up wishing the opposite on your children. Being someone who can't be bothered, who shirks effort, is no good. It's no good, and it shrinks your own possibilities. If only I'd been the kind of person who didn't hold back, I'd have had a better life... that's the sort of thing I find myself thinking.

And yet, in reality, other people describe me as the exact opposite: conscientious, eager to have a go at anything. If anything, I suspect they take me for a hard worker. But as far as I'm concerned, I find everything a bother, I begrudge every ounce of effort, I'm short on motivation, slow to get off my backside, and I'd rather be asleep, rather be lolling about. I feel as though I've lived alongside that frame of mind my whole life. That feeling is where I really come from, the true head of the household. And yet somehow, I keep ending up doing things. I even catch myself hurrying along through my ordinary days. Why on earth?!

Even today, even right now as I write this, I'm thinking what a bother it is. And yet I don't stop writing, and I've been writing every single day for more than twenty-eight years. If someone like me turned into a genuine "person who doesn't find things a bother," would I end up busier than ever with one thing and another? Hmm, that might not be such a good thing. If I went around playing at being a racing driver with my foot flat to the floor, wouldn't I wreck my own body and make a nuisance of myself to everyone around me? That's the kind of excuse I come up with, but even that seems a little suspect. I admire people who never act as though things are a bother, oh, I really, truly do. I think they're wonderful, I think they're cool, I long to be like them. And yet, somehow, I'm just not one of them.
