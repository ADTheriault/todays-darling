## Title: A cry from the heart: "Don't underestimate me!"
## Date: 2026-08-17
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

The way I put this could rub people the wrong way, so I have to choose my words carefully. From something as vast as a nation or a cultural sphere, down to the grouchy old man next door or some random young guy on the street, the demand of "Don't underestimate me!" might just be, when you get right down to it, stronger, deeper, and more fierce than any other desire we carry.

If you take whatever people are arguing for in a quarrel or a moment of anger and boil it down to its very essence, it comes out as "Don't underestimate me!" Whether it's a sweeping historical epic, a domestic family drama, a war film, or a superhero story, what runs beneath any situation involving conflict is, more or less, this same theme.

The cry of "Don't underestimate me!" gets generally filed under words like "pride" or "self-respect," but it's not something you can tidy away into a noun like that. "Don't underestimate me!" isn't a concept, it's the cry of raw emotion. It can't be reasoned out in terms of gain and loss, and no amount of "because it's the right thing" will make it sit quietly.

To force others to acknowledge that "Don't underestimate me!", a person will seemingly do anything, without even sparing their own life. To make that feeling of "Don't underestimate me!" into something genuine, people work hard, endure, and pour out their blood and sweat. In a certain sense, they'll even steel themselves to be underestimated. So yes, there's actually good that comes out of "Don't underestimate me!" too.

But here's the thing. If you loosen the screws on that "Don't underestimate me!" signboard, just a little at a time, what you get in return, oddly enough, is "freedom." Between not being underestimated and being free, if you ask which is better, I'd say it's probably the latter. Though that part, I suppose, comes down to how each person chooses to live.
