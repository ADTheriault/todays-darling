## Title: What's wrong with you, pal (yes, you, Itoi)
## Date: 2026-10-10
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

There's something I've been wanting to tell somebody flat out: "This is your fault!" And that somebody is me.

Sit down right there. We need to talk. Now why would you go and sit down clutching your phone? You expecting some urgent call to come in while I'm talking to you? Of course you're not. Has that ever happened, even once? What I want to talk about is nothing other than that phone of yours. You keep it glued to you, twenty-four hours a day, three hundred sixty-five days a year. Somewhere along the line you started doing this thing where you get up to use the bathroom and go, "Whoops, forgot my phone," and march right back for it. If you're going to go that far, why not just stitch the thing onto your body? Not a tattoo, exactly, but close enough. No? What's that, it'd be hard to charge if it were sewn on? Well, figure something out.

No, no, that's not what I meant to get into. Get away from it, that's what I'm trying to tell you. Go ahead and "confiscate" it yourself, if that's what it takes. Then you couldn't buy a train ticket or an ice cream, you say? Nobody's telling you to stop carrying it when you actually need it. What's wrong is the nonstop part. You got some disease where you'll drop dead if you're not staring at that screen? Didn't think so. You're just hooked on the thing, that's all. All that "So handy, so handy, what a blessing, what a blessing" business, that's how people thought ages ago, back when these things first came out and were still a novelty. Cramming everything in through your eyes and kidding yourself that you know it all, where does that get you?

Look, I know you're not some kid with a game console, but you need a rule, something like "Phone time: two hours a day, max." Hm? What are you supposed to do if you're not looking at your phone? Get rid of it first, then worry about that. You never had one to begin with, so you won't be stuck. Just sit there idle with your mouth hanging open, staring into space. What's wrong with that? Draw some Chiikawa, read a book, whatever. Got it? No more extra phone time. That's final.
