## Title: Me in this vast world.
## Date: 2026-09-29
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

The mosquito that lands on your cheek is fiercer than any flying dragon at the edge of the earth. The wailing of your son claws at the heart more than the song of the Sirens. (from <em>The Dead End Road</em>)

The things that happen right around us are, for a single human being, greater than anything else. And yet, while I say this, what I'm about to talk about is no bigger than the tip of a fingernail. No, really, it is about the tips of fingernails.

For many years I lived as a devoted nail-clipper enthusiast, but for some months now I haven't touched a nail clipper at all. I just buff down whatever has grown with a glass nail file. I started it half-heartedly, telling myself I'd quit when I got bored or go back to the old way when it became a bother, but I've kept it up this whole time without stopping. Only, lately I've switched from the glass file to a pen-shaped product called the "Nail King," which is simpler and doesn't scatter the shavings. Who would have thought a day would come when I'd file down my grown nails instead of using scissors or clippers.

This next thing might seem even smaller, but at long last the announcement has gone out for "Shigesato Itoi's Live Reading."

We tried it once as a test, and it was genuinely fun. All it involves is reading a book and sharing my impressions in front of only my own colleagues, family really. What do people think about while they're reading a book? I decided to make myself the guinea pig and find out. The problem is that it will expose the fact that "I'm not thinking about anything much." I'm no scholar or expert, so I simply read. Books aren't written by logic to begin with. Reading is a person writing and a person reading, plain and simple. Most little arguments and hair-splitting come after the fact anyway. In the spirit of "life really begins once your stupidity is out in the open," I intend to do this live, as just one of the little folk.
