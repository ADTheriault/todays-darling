## Title: Keep saying that and you'll get stalked.
## Date: 2026-08-09
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Hot again today, isn't it. But you know, no matter how much you complain about the heat, it doesn't make the heat go away. All that muttering about how hot it is just fixes your mind on it, and then you can't stop thinking about the heat all day long. You spend your whole day haunted by the spirit that lives inside those words. And whoever happens to be nearby gets dragged into it too, chased around by "it's so hot, it's so hot" for no fault of their own.

It's the same with everything, really. There are people who do nothing but grumble that they've got no money. And it's never the ones who actually have none, it's the ones who just love to say it. Does the money rain down from heaven because you keep saying you haven't got any? Is somebody going to hand you cash just because you claim to be broke? If a fellow opens his mouth only to say he's skint, skint, skint, then folks around him figure they'll never see their money again if they lend it, and they clam up their wallets. This sort of thing pulls other people in too, so you really ought to watch yourself.

Unpleasant things, worries, there's no end of them. Even when it looks like you've got none, go looking and you'll find some. And what pleasure is there in dredging that stuff up over and over, making other people sigh? Well, I suppose they do it because they like doing it. "Even the heat gets forgiven only three times," same as the patience of a saint, and that's about right.

That's the sort of thing I meant to write, but whenever I set it down the usual way I end up holding back and softening it. So today I channeled the spirit of the master storyteller Tatekawa Danshi and spoke through him.

Now then, a little more about the "100 Questions Interview." I'm grateful for it, because people share what they've been thinking, and it makes me feel as though I'm meeting and talking with lots of people every day.

◆Today's is no. 005, "Can you swim?" Nice and simple. It's summer, after all, so have some fun with this one. Jot your thoughts and feelings down in your notebook and let them accumulate. And if you feel like it, write them in an email and send them my way.

Thank you for coming to Hobonichi again today. As for me, I went to the movies at night and watched "Chiikawa" for the third time.
