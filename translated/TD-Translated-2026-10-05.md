## Title: Meeting by chance and simply talking.
## Date: 2026-10-05
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I've probably had more chances than most people to meet others and talk with them. Some I know well, some I know only a little, and sometimes I meet people I hardly know at all. It's not as if I show up having done zero research on them, zero thought about what we might talk about. That would be too much. But I make a point of not "researching thoroughly" or "preparing carefully."

If someone wants to prepare for something "thoroughly," by all means, go ahead. On one condition, though: that they're ready to let all that enormous preparation go to waste.

Map out the route too precisely and it's guaranteed to be no fun. When two people meet, it's the moment a kind of "living thing" is born. If both sides just take turns reciting things they already know perfectly well, it's hard for anything to come of it, and honestly, the conversation itself isn't interesting while it's happening. You might come away thinking you "learned something," sure. But I think what's most interesting is when both people stumble onto some small new discovery together.

I once explained this way of meeting people to the other person directly. I was about to have a conversation with someone from overseas whom I'd never met, and I started by saying, "Shall we talk like two strangers who just happened to end up sitting next to each other at a bar?" Even with a bit of prior knowledge, asking "So, where are you from?" all over again is exactly the sort of thing a neighbor at a bar might do. I've kept doing this kind of thing over and over, and it's made me who I am today.

That said, this thing I'm writing here, the former "Today's Darling," who exactly am I writing it for, and what am I actually doing? Maybe it's like the owner of some little shop making small talk with a customer from down the street, going, "Seems like there's a lot of pollen in the air lately, huh..." Which kind of roasted sweet potato is the tastiest, a good manga I came across, and sometimes, with a perfectly straight face, something solemn and self-important too.

◆Today's question is no. 062: "Anything good happen to you lately?" Whether something did or didn't, it's all just small talk. Let me know by email.
