## Title: Come on in. Thanks, as always. Say, you know what?
## Date: 2026-10-17
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

This column I write every day, the one that used to be called "Today's Darling," has covered all sorts of things from all sorts of angles. Back in 1998, when we'd only just launched Hobonichi, I'd already realized, "...I've got nothing to write about," and I once simply described the view outside my window. Or at least I think I did. I've forgotten.

For a while there, I vaguely came to think of these scribblings as something like a newspaper editorial, and I found myself wanting to make some small point or other. What pushed me into that frame of mind was, naturally, the period that began with the Great East Japan Earthquake on March 11, 2011. The tsunami surged onto the Sanriku coast, yes, but the sea of the internet was flooded too, with a mass of troubling information. Rumors, lies, abuse, slander, noise. I was swimming for dear life, colliding with all that floating debris. Even so, used the right way, the internet could be genuinely helpful in many respects, and that desperate swim taught me a great deal. I learned, to some extent, how to keep a sensible distance from it.

Be that as it may, I write this piece every single day. Sometimes it's a matter of trying one trick after another, sometimes I'm thrashing around in agony, sometimes I'm half sprawled out and nodding off, and sometimes I charge in, thrilled, because there's one thing I absolutely have to say. Whatever my attitude, here I am again today, writing something.

Lately, I've had a "concept" for writing here. It occurred to me one day, and I decided to run with it for a while. It was also where Hobonichi started, back at the very beginning. The idea is this: "the old guy who shows his face at the shop every single day strikes up some kind of conversation with you." Today's piece is that kind of chatter, too. He starts in with, "Hey there, how're you doing? Say, lately..." And one of these days, who knows, he might even come out with, "I thought of a good pun. Want to hear it?"

◆ Today's question, no. 074: "Do you have a favorite place you keep going back to?" Have a think, and if you like, write it in an email and send it our way.

Thank you for stopping by Hobonichi again today. For quite a long time now, I've been writing this column in a frame of 27 characters by 40 lines.
