## Title: Meeting young people never stops being interesting
## Date: 2026-10-22
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

The things that get better have gotten better. And the things that get clumsier have gotten clumsier.

Things that everybody used to know are, little by little, known by fewer people than not. And plenty of things that nobody used to know are now known by a great many.

What on earth would become of the world if this were gone? Then it was gone, and the world kept right on turning.

The record set by someone hailed as a once-in-a-generation genius was broken by someone you'd barely heard of. That dish that left you thunderstruck, wondering how anything this delicious could exist in the world, maybe wasn't quite all that after all.

Young girls grow into middle-aged women, and then into old women. Dashing young men grow into middle-aged men, and then into old men. Even a crane that lives a thousand years passes on once the thousand years are up. And the turtle that lives ten thousand years will, when those years have run their course, surely find its rest as well.

None of this is hollow. It isn't something to regret, and it isn't something to grieve. Until the real end comes, there is no end. While you've got energy, you go at it with energy, and when you don't, you manage as best you can.

The things that get better have gotten better. And the things that go bad have gone bad. I suppose what I really want to say is this: the things that get better are getting better.
