## Title: Enjoying the joys of a hot spring in your own bath?
## Date: 2026-09-12
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I sink slowly into the bath at home, thinking, ah, there's nothing like a good soak, and yet I find myself wondering why on earth this isn't the same as a hot spring. Lately I've been adding "Kuma's Recommended Hot Spring Essence," made from refined mineral bath salts, so the water I'm soaking in feels awfully close to the real thing. I've even set the temperature a touch on the hot side, going by my memories of actual hot springs. If you consider only the relationship between my body and the water, I really do think this is the same as a hot spring.

You know how, a little while after you've been to a hot spring, you notice, oh, my body's still warm and toasty? I think you really are warmed through, right down inside. And with a bath at home, the kind with the spring essence added, you get something pretty close to that same afterglow. Then there's that feeling while you're actually soaking in the spring water, that sense of, mmm, this is warming me right up, the hot spring is seeping into me. I feel that at home too, in the bath with the essence in it. At this point I'd really like to say they're the same thing.

I'd love to, but the truth is a real hot spring is still better! The bathroom at home looks exactly like the bathroom at home. No matter how expensive the tiles you line it with, it's the family bathroom. There's a real difference between that and stepping into a genuine hot spring where the whole "now this is a hot spring" scenery spreads out before your eyes.

So how could I possibly capture the feeling of a hot spring at home? I kept turning this over in my mind, staring at the dull tiled wall of my own bathroom, and recently I reached a conclusion. What if I simply practiced "getting into the right frame of mind"? If, with my eyes closed, I can convince myself, this is a hot spring, ahhh, this feels wonderful, then it already is a hot spring. And if I can't quite convince myself, well, I'll just keep practicing until I can! That way, I should be able to savor the joy of a hot spring at home every single day. Being the fellow who's lately made a point of "cultivating his subjectivity," I shall surely succeed in luring a hot spring here, free of charge and requiring no equipment whatsoever.
