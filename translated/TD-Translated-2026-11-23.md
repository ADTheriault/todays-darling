## Title: Respect, but not submission
## Date: 2026-11-23
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

You mustn't take it lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by it. Honestly, you can say that about most things.

Take the economy, for instance. You mustn't take it lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by it. By which I mean money, profit, that sort of thing. You can flip the order, too: you mustn't be held captive by the economy, and you mustn't take it lightly.

Interestingly, almost everything people speak of as important fits this phrasing.

You mustn't take freedom lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by freedom. Both happen all the time.

You mustn't take family lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by family. I think there's plenty of both.

You mustn't take love lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by love. Either way, it's no good.

You mustn't take knowledge lightly, and you mustn't be held captive by knowledge. You see both of these often enough.

Hold respect for the things that matter, but don't submit to them. That's the attitude. I'm making it today's title.
