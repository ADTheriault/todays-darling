## Title: I love ten-thousand-yen bills.
## Date: 2026-10-08
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

At that place you love and I love too, around 11:15 in the morning, I joined the line of people waiting for lunch. There's a long bench, so all you have to do is sit and read a book. While you're waiting, someone comes to ask how many you are and what you'd like to eat, and by the time the line moves and you can go inside, your table is ready. Better still, the cooking starts while you're still in line, so not long after you sit down, your food arrives. Cold water in a clean glass, and a little dish of pickled mustard greens. Then the main dish, rice, and miso soup. You eat, and it's delicious, no question about it, really good. You catch the eye of one of the cooks you've come to know, and you share a small smile. That smile means "it's delicious, I'm happy."

It's by no means a new place, so the floors and walls must be old, and yet, here's the thing, they're polished to a shine. On this particular day, I noticed the ceiling lights reflecting off the floor. They don't make a show of their service at all. They aren't rushing around frantically, but they're quick. They aren't exactly all smiles, either, and yet you feel so welcome. I once asked, "Is everyone who works here related?" The answer was no, not at all, we're all strangers. I suppose this is what good teamwork looks like. The prices aren't especially cheap, but they aren't expensive either. As for the food, it isn't the kind of deliciousness that makes you want to go on about it, like "one bite of this and you'll faint." It's just always good, served fast and never sloppy.

All of this, by the way, is about my usual haunt, "Fumin."

When it came time to pay, I found I had no thousand-yen notes in my wallet. "Oh, I'm so sorry, all I've got is a ten-thousand-yen note," I said, and the manager, in that wonderfully cheerful voice of his, shot back, "Of course, I love ten-thousand-yen notes!" I think that's a great line, born from years and years of experience. And really, isn't it just the best? "I love ten-thousand-yen notes!" Without thinking, I replied, "I love them too."
