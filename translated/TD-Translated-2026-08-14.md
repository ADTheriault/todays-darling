## Title: Is it okay to just be vaguely happy?
## Date: 2026-08-14
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I've been thinking about how to spend what people call the "Obon holiday," and I've come to a certain place. Both my wife and I have lost our parents, so neither of us has a family home to return to anymore. In a sense, I'm the family home now. People like us are free to spend Obon wherever we please. Then again, there's no need to make such a grand thing of it.

There's that expression, "this town is my second home," and it occurred to me how fortunate it would be to actually have such a place. And when I came here, I realized, ah, it isn't as though I don't have one. It's not that there's anything special here (which is a rude way to put it), but something about it, it just puts me at ease. There's no sightseeing to speak of. I move about a little, eat a meal at that one restaurant, catch sight of that one person's face, and that's about it.

The inn has a hot spring, so of course I'll be soaking in it. We arrived, greeted the people who invited us, shared a meal, and already I'm content. There's nothing else I need to do. Tomorrow there's a little "tourist spot" we'll drop by, but it's nothing to get worked up over. If it turns out nice, well, that's nice, and that's all there is to it.

We're here with my daughter's family, which of course means my daughter's daughter is here too. Ever since we were on the bullet train, I'd been sneaking glances at my granddaughter in the seat behind us, thoroughly enjoying myself. It was a bit like watching Panchan at the Ichikawa zoo. Just observing her is entertaining in itself. Forgive me, sweetheart, you're already thinking all sorts of grown-up thoughts, and here I am treating you like a child, comparing you to a little monkey.

Naturally, the conversation turns to "Chiikawa" as well. Of everyone gathered here, the two who know it best are this seven-year-old girl and me, at seventy-seven. Across a seventy-year gap in age, a proper Chiikawa discussion takes place. How good it is that this movie came out during the summer holidays. And how good it is that this town, my "second home," exists.
