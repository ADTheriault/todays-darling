## Title: It started with saying cold at Akagi, and wandered on from there...
## Date: 2026-10-23
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

For the first time in a while, I went to "Hobo no Eki AKAGI." Looking back, it was a real shame I never made it there in summer, when it's at its best as a place to escape the heat. Now isn't the season for that. People come mostly for the autumn leaves and for the views under clear fall skies. It was a weekday, yet quite a few guests were there, taking it easy.

Being there, I remembered a word I'd forgotten. "Cold." I said it out loud, and I put on an extra layer.

I'm hopeless with the cold. Every year, when the weather turns, I think of the rakugo story "The Clam Seller." It opens with a small child wading barefoot into an icy river to gather clams, which he sells to help his poor family scrape by. Heat is hard too, of course, but cold is what really gets to me. We talked endlessly about heatstroke this summer, and I've already forgotten all of it. With cold, there's a fear underneath: this will kill you. People go cold when they die, don't they? Cold is a matter of life and death. Every year, as winter draws near, I start complaining about the cold. Winter has its good points, and I've made it through dozens of them by now. Still, there's a part of me that hates the cold even more than the heat.

Speaking of things I hate, and this comes out of nowhere, I hate torture. When I was very young, a few of us were chatting about what we each couldn't stand, and I think I gave it real thought and settled on torture. I won't go into the specifics of why here, but torture is the very definition of "something you just don't do." Torture means imagining what a person would least want done to them, and then doing it (which is why the torturer's own weakness is hidden inside the torture itself). I hate the idea of bending people to your will through things that must never be done. I'd count threats and coercion as forms of torture too. Torture is trying to strip a person of even their most basic freedom.

The word "cold," spoken on a clear autumn day among the red leaves of Akagi, has somehow turned into talk of clam sellers and torture. Free as a drunk, wandering wherever it pleases, I suppose.
