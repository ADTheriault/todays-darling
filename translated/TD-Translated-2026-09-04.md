## Title: Is there someone you'd want to become if you could?
## Date: 2026-09-04
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

The older guys back in the day had this habit of comparing themselves to warlords from the Sengoku era. "See, our department head, he's a Tokugawa Ieyasu, so..." or "Well, I'm an Oda Nobunaga myself, patience was never my thing." Honestly, I wanted to tell them, look, you're not Ieyasu and you're not Nobunaga, you're just you, plain and simple. But there's no sense ruining a good story, so I kept my mouth shut.

Then there were all the Sakamoto Ryomas, scattered across every corner of the country. So many of them. I'm Ryoma, he's Ryoma, everybody's Ryoma. You could practically hold a "Sakamoto Ryoma Convention" or start a "Sakamoto Ryoma Party." And since the man himself is dead serious about it, I figured I might as well let him keep talking like some noble revolutionary from a period drama.

Oh, and there was the Beatles version too. "I'm the Paul of the group, so I'm the one smoothing things over." "Me, I just can't help being a John." You didn't hear "I'm the Ringo" or "I'm the George" nearly as often. Whatever the case, buddy, you're not John, you're not Paul, and you're not Ringo either.

I wonder what happened to those "imaginary heroes." Somewhere along the way, whether we let it slip on purpose or just by accident, they seem to have vanished from the world. Maybe it's because the moment anyone starts talking with that kind of feverish enthusiasm, some cool, sober voice cuts them right down. Maybe everyone's simply figured out that, at the end of the day, I'm just me and nothing more. Or maybe the whole world has just gotten a bit too "clever." And sure, it's true, you aren't Nobunaga, and you aren't Paul. You'll never be Shohei Ohtani either. ♫

I have absolutely no intention of saying this state of affairs means we've lost our dreams. After all, I couldn't tell you what the dreams of all those Sakamoto Ryomas actually were.

Ah, that's it, let's make this today's theme for the reader interview.

◆Question no. 031: "Is there someone you'd want to become, if you could?"
