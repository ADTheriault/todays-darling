## Title: The Hobonichi in the station is a little different.
## Date: 2026-09-18
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

A "Hobonichi Techo Store" has opened inside Tokyo Station. When I say "inside," I mean within the station itself, which means you pass through the ticket gates to get to it. Having a shop of our own "inside a station" was something I (personally) had never even imagined. The plans had been in motion for quite a while, and now, at last, it's open.

My apologies, but until September 19th (Saturday), entry is by lottery reservation only, and from the following day it opens as an ordinary shop. It is inside a station, after all. The hours are long, from ten in the morning until nine at night. And why is that? Again, because it's inside a station.

There really is something special about being inside Tokyo Station. The staff rotations must be quite different from an ordinary workplace, I found myself saying, chatting away like the complete amateur I am. And then something else occurred to me. "Come to think of it, what happens during summer holidays, or New Year's?" I blurted out, rather absentmindedly.

"There aren't any. It's Tokyo Station." The answer came back at once. I could understand the logic well enough, but it struck me that this was the first workplace I'd ever heard of like it. Of course, no single person works there all year round. Even so, the "place" itself never rests. That's what makes it different from an ordinary shop. Yes, yes, I understand, individuals get their days off, properly arranged. Railways and police forces surely operate the same way.

Still, the idea of maintaining a "place that never closes" was something I'd never been able to picture before. "Wow, so that's what they've been doing. What a noble thing," I said, once again sounding like a schoolchild fresh off a social studies field trip.

And then they said it to me. "Itoi-san, you've been running completely every single day yourself, haven't you?" For more than twenty-eight years, it's true, I've kept writing this, day after day.
