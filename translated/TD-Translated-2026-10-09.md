## Title: What is the miracle hero thinking?
## Date: 2026-10-09
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Every now and then, I find myself thinking about Shohei Ohtani's season this year. As a pitcher, he went 8 and 2 with a 1.79 ERA. Those are terrific numbers; any team would take as many pitchers like that as it could get. As a hitter, he had 144 hits in 523 at-bats for a .275 average, with 81 RBIs and 30 home runs, which also puts him among the best on his club. And yet, next to the "superhuman" performances we've grown used to, people can't help feeling that even this falls short.

Midway through the season, trouble with his left knee forced him to stop pitching, and later came the announcement of inflammation in his biceps. That affected his hitting as well, and it ended any chance of his returning to the mound. There were also reports that his neck had been bothering him. In September, he went on the 15-day injured list. He made it back in time for the postseason, but the results haven't come the way anyone hoped. The team is winning, but the highlights we see aren't of Ohtani in full flight. They're of other players stepping up. I haven't seen this kind of "team with Shohei Ohtani on it" in years, and it gives me a strange feeling.

The injuries and slumps that most professional ballplayers go through came for the "miracle hero" too. Or did they? Maybe he'll bounce right back. The answer is still out of reach, and it isn't as if anyone else knows it either. The road to the World Series has, after all, only just begun.

So what becomes of this "unicorn" named Shohei Ohtani, the man people called "not human," too much even for a manga? If this were a sweeping historical drama, we'd be at the dark climax right now, the low point before the turn. When things are going badly, when the odds are against him, what does he think about, what does he work on, and how does he find his way out and then explode? A story we've never seen before has already begun. I'd love to know how Ohtani is feeling right now, even if I only find out later.
