## Title: Writing this down as a warning to myself (ha)
## Date: 2026-11-05
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Maybe parents and teachers are different these days, but for my generation, and for a generation or so after mine, adults were forever handing out warnings. "Don't get full of yourself." "Don't get complacent." "Nothing is ever easy." "You're not trying hard enough." "Don't you dare take this lightly." We heard it constantly. Having spent my life steeped up to the neck in admonitions meant to keep me from going easy on myself, I eventually got into the habit of telling myself not to get full of myself. Whenever I set out to do something, I furrow my brow and think, "This is going to be hard." Even when things are going well, I lecture myself: "Get cocky and you'll fall flat on your face." I'm not saying this about the world at large. That stuff is sloshing around inside me, filled right to the brim. That's how I was raised, and that's how I've lived.

Still, if you live like that, you never get anything done. And I didn't want to become the kind of person who's always frightening or scolding other people about whatever they're trying to do. So I've gone through life tossing off jokes like, "Is the world sweet or sour? You'll never know until you give it a lick."

Calmly scanning for oversights, for hidden pitfalls, makes a person look smart, so plenty of people are eager to do it. And since everyone dreads hearing "I told you so" when they fail, clever people like to be the ones doing the checking. But there's no point in having everybody crowded over on that side. I'll say it again: I'm prone to ending up on that side myself.

That's not the way! Something that seems difficult can turn out to be the other side of the very same coin as something you can do if you just try. You shouldn't take things lightly, but you won't get anywhere if you're afraid. Even the work you do with a furrowed brow can be done while laughing. For some reason, I felt like writing all that down again.
