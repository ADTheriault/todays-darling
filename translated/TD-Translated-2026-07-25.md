## Title: Breathe in, then eat.
## Date: 2026-07-25
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I've felt this for a long time, but I could never quite put my finger on it. The question of whether I've always done things this way. And by "this," I mean the technique of eating while inhaling.

The classic example is a cream puff. Bite into one the normal way, and the cream is guaranteed to come squirting out. And as for the pastry shell, well, you can't get a tooth or a knife into it cleanly. So I'd wonder, how on earth is everyone else eating these things, while I quietly sucked out the cream, nibbling a bit of the shell as I inhaled.

It's strange. I'm fully aware that what I'm doing is very strange. But short of that, how else is a person supposed to eat one? Just scoop out the cream with a spoon, you say? With all due respect, do you actually do that? Is that really how you eat them? You buy cream puffs at the convenience store, don't you. You pick one up as a snack at a place built for convenience, and then you reach for a spoon? I just don't think that's what they're for. Surely there's no choice but to suck.

By the same logic, what do you do about a big hamburger? Not the plain, simple kind, but the indulgent sort. A proper gourmet burger, the kind where the juices come dripping down. The vegetables and the meat go sliding out and land who knows where. Even with the wrapper braced around it to keep the filling in, it's still hard to just chomp down with a wide-open mouth. Do that, and you're bound to drop something, juices included. So here too, isn't sucking the only option? I do it. Not because I want to, but I suck.

What about an egg sandwich, stuffed thick with egg? The more generously filled a thing is, the more it spills. So don't agonize over this one either. Just suck. I suck.

The cream puff, the hamburger, the egg sandwich, and while we're at it, the jam bun and the cream bun too. In the old days we ate these without a second thought and never spilled a drop, didn't we. Hadn't you noticed? These foods, nowadays, spill precisely because they're so generously filled. It's abundance that turned me into someone who sucks.

Thank you for coming to Hobonichi today as well. And please don't say "so what." That's all there is to it.
