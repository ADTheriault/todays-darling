## Title: Do you like "like"?
## Date: 2026-08-28
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

A film about somebody loving somebody else tends to leave you feeling good. Ah, but maybe that's not always true. It isn't always true, and yet, more often than not, surprisingly, it turns out to be a fine thing.

Not just love stories, either. The feeling you have for a friend, for the people around you. A gentle tenderness toward small things. A liking that's almost like longing. A liking you never quite realize is liking.

In the reality of living, what does a person actually do for the one they care about? What form does that feeling take? These are things we rarely get to see. Now and then we catch a glimpse, sure.

When you've come to like someone, how do you feel? What do you find yourself wanting to do? You put that feeling out into the open, then pull it back in again. The only records of such things exist in two places: inside stories, and inside yourself.

How do people who like each other behave? While we watch it unfold in a story, we find ourselves remembering our own feelings of liking. Not just love stories, but anywhere that liking is expressed, it comes back to us.

That we exist, and that within us there is this thing called liking. It would be good to have many chances to remember that. A world where dislike is easier to find than liking is not a particularly happy one to live in.
