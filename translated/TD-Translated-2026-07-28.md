## Title: A story about not being able to sleep, a story about not caring, and a story about nightmares.
## Date: 2026-07-28
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

I was talking with an acquaintance who likes to boast about how badly he sleeps. Before long, we discovered that he and I were taking the very same sleeping pill. They say misery loves company, and it's true, there's a small pleasure in finding a fellow sufferer like this.

At the time, though, I had been practicing how to get by without the drug, and I'd been having pretty good luck with it. I'll probably manage to fall asleep tonight, too, without taking anything.

"Oh, come on, don't say that, let's just take it," my kindred spirit in misery kept wanting to say. He really is a good man, but he has this side to him. It was the same when he was quitting cigarettes. He came up with every excuse in the book and dragged it out forever. I'd had a rough time of it myself when I quit smoking, so if I could, I wanted to tell him that quitting would actually make life easier. But well, he's not the kind of man you can tie down that neatly. In the end he only quit after a doctor gave him a serious scare. And now he's saying the same sort of thing about sleeping pills.

The reason I've been able to stop taking the drug is, honestly, thanks to controlling my own "perspective." I simply reconsidered the way I thought about insomnia. "Don't fret about not being able to sleep." "Just lying down with your eyes closed is enough."

(We talked for a good while longer after this.)

And then the conversation turned, in that unmistakably nerdy, deep-in-the-weeds way, to how "an incredibly advanced new sleeping pill has been developed." All sorts of wonderfully convenient improvements had been built into it, apparently.

"The thing is, its one drawback is that it seems to give you nightmares." Ugh, nightmares, I'd hate that. Please, spare me the nightmares. "I have real reservations about that too. That's why I can't quite bring myself to try it." Right, right, I understand completely.

"But you know, the doctor who told me about it... he said something." Oh? What did the doctor say? "'So you have a nightmare, what of it? They're interesting, aren't they.'"

Whoa! So this too comes down to controlling your "perspective"!
