## Title: The flow of things: hard to explain, and that's the fun of it
## Date: 2026-11-21
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

There's this thing called "the flow," isn't there? As in "the flow of the game," or "we need to change the flow..." Everyone involved gets swept up in a shared picture, one with no real basis but heavy with a sense of what's coming. "When things are like this, this is how they go," and somehow everybody just decides it together. The flow is a story we're expecting, and sometimes it's also a dream we've seen many times before.

But even when things don't go the way the flow foretold, nobody seems to mind much. "The flow was pointing the other way, you know," people say, explaining the result after the fact. There was never any basis for it to begin with, so that's fine.

I'm rather fond of this faith in the flow. There's the pleasure of giving yourself over to it, and the pleasure of resisting it. It's a bit like saying "it was meant to be" or "I felt the hand of fate." When you sense a good flow and decide to ride it, the flow often seems to get even better. Probably because other people are sensing that "good flow" too, and they try to help it along. And then, if I'm being level-headed about it, when what was supposed to be a "good flow" ends in failure, people pick themselves up with an "Oh well" or a "So close." "We got pretty far, though," that sort of thing. Maybe I've simply "flowed" my way here myself, without ever really working out what I think about the flow at all.

It's nothing terribly important, but right now there's a big flow running through my life. A flow has come along urging me to walk. From books, from shoes, from horoscopes, from what the people around me are up to, there's a flow telling me I ought to walk more. If I make a grand resolution and set off with gusto, I'll get bored in no time, so I'm easing into it slowly, like soaking my feet in a footbath of that flow. It isn't a bad thing, after all, so I'm going to ride it and see where it goes. Only, now that I'm finally in the mood, the winter cold has come rushing in, as if to push back against the current.
