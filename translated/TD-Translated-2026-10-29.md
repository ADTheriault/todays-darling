## Title: The kind of thing even I'd forget if I didn't write it down
## Date: 2026-10-29
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

What I'm about to write is truly trivial, utterly beside the point. It's just that if I don't write it, it will stay unwritten forever, and somehow that felt like a bit of a shame, so I decided to write it. What's more, I made this resolution while squatting on the toilet... a real bottom-line decision, you might say.

Here goes: I want a bread machine again. Not a toaster. I mean the electric gadget that bakes bread starting from flour. There may still be one lying around somewhere in the house, but I suspect the one I bought last time has been thrown out. Naturally, since it sat unused for years and years. I can't imagine that my boss at home, who is enshrined here as the Deity of Discarding, would have let it sit there unnoticed.

That's right, I've bought one before. Actually, I bought one before that, too. Over the course of my long life, I've bought maybe five bread machines... or I might be padding the count by one. Let's say four times, then, I bought one, got bored with it, and it was either forgotten or thrown away.

And now I've stumbled on a reason to want one, just a little. A specialty baking-supplies shop was selling "bread mix" flour. Oh, so you can buy the flour here, I thought. I bet it's delicious. And the instant that tiny thought crossed my mind, the smell of fresh-baked bread came billowing through my brain. It doesn't keep well, but straight out of the machine, it's the best. And then the bonus came charging in: "It makes mochi, too!" I mean, come on. Now I'm hungry for it. That's just where I'm at.

It isn't even expensive, only a little over ten thousand yen. If I were an old man living alone, I'd buy one this very minute. But I'm not, and my boss reads these ramblings too. "Obviously not! Honestly, the very idea!" So I'm not buying one. A bread machine? Absolutely not. And even if, hypothetically, I did buy one, I'd hide it somewhere and, picking a moment when the boss is out, quickly bake a loaf on the sly. Though, you know, I don't think the boss would actually object to eating it. Maybe I'll borrow one from someone at Hobonichi, a bread machine...
