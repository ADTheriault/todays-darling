## Title: On getting along
## Date: 2026-10-30
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

You know those videos where a mother dog or cat introduces her babies to a person she's come to trust? I've seen a few of them. I can't know what she's actually feeling, but from our side, the human side, I can't help reading it my own way: "I wanted you to meet them. These are my little ones, my precious, precious babies. Please be good to them." It looks as though she's bringing together the children she treasures and someone she knows she can rely on.

We're always being encouraged to mix: across industries, across cultures, to bring different kinds of people together. And why is it so pleasant to watch creatures of different species getting along? Unlikely pairs of animals playing together, eating together, one nursing the other's young... When a video shows me that yes, this really can happen, I can't help feeling glad.

Mind you, animals of the same kind getting along warm the heart plenty, too. Yes, I suppose anything getting along stirs up a kind of pleasure in whoever's watching. Watching children play together makes me happy somehow, and it's lovely to see a child doting on a pet or a doll.

Everybody loves seeing others get along. But, well, in real life, getting along doesn't seem all that easy. Not getting along looks like the simpler, safer choice, and perhaps it even pays off now and then. I wonder whether getting along is something a person can learn.
