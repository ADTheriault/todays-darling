## Title: There's nothing I can do about myself.
## Date: 2026-10-01
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Yikes.

It's just dawned on me, October 1st is going to be a handful. I'm sitting down to write this in the middle of the night, as always, but tomorrow morning begins with the one thing I'm no good at: getting up early. I've got a CT scan scheduled, so it's off to the hospital first thing. I booked it so long ago I'd completely forgotten about it. Then in the afternoon, slotted in among the usual work, there's one of those appointments for my so-called "workout." Part of me thinks skipping it would make life easier, but no, it's the opposite. On a day like this, moving the body is probably exactly what I need. So I'll go, grumbling the whole way, and do it properly.

The real problem comes after that.

"Shigesato Itoi's Live Reading #1" was looming on the schedule.

Why on earth did I pick this day? I could ask the telephone pole, but it wouldn't know. Ask it where it lives, no answer. Ask it its name, no answer. Just a little cat, mewing to itself.

There's one precedent, so I know from experience: this takes an enormous amount of stamina and nerve. I may make it look like I'm just fluttering along, breezy and effortless, but you'd understand soon enough if you tried it yourself. The reading parts I have to do in my own voice, too, so honestly, I'm worried about whether I can even pull it off. In case you've forgotten, I'm past sixty. Day to day, I'm a doddering old man shuffling about. There'll be kanji I can't read, and my thinking isn't exactly deep either.

But then again, this whole thing was my idea in the first place, and the person in charge is that most fearsome Ms. N. So-and-so, which means there's no escaping it. Maybe I'll keep some of that powdered Ryukakusan throat remedy from the aluminum can on hand. And once we wrap the recording, maybe I'll go grab some chive soba or something. I have no idea how many hours this is going to take. A Shincho Bunko paperback, just a short story, but sixty-seven pages... oh dear.

Live on.
