## Title: Messing up and having fun.
## Date: 2026-10-07
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

Every now and then, not often, but it happens, I mess things up. Say I've personally promised someone, "Let's meet up," and I jot it down only in my own private schedule. If I forget to report it to K, the god of my calendar, the result is always some not so small inconvenience for everyone involved. And since these promises tend to be for the evening, I sometimes end up eating dinner twice, two suppers in one night. But let's set that aside for now.

・

When I started a project called <em>Shigesato Itoi's Live Reading</em>, it turned out to be two solid hours of reading aloud and sharing impressions, nonstop talking that left me utterly exhausted in body and spirit. And yet, somehow, I found myself strangely enjoying it. I'd decided to do one trial run, then one proper first session, and if it seemed impossible I could just quit. But even if it is impossible, I came away wanting to do the next one.

If I do say so myself, the book selection was excellent. Naoya Shiga's <em>The Shopboy's God</em>, Osamu Dazai's <em>Click-Clack Mountain</em>, both so wonderful as fiction that they're deeply rewarding to read. What made the "Live Reading" so enjoyable was, first and foremost, that the stories themselves were wonderful. For the first time in ages, I remembered the simple pleasure of just reading a novel, not some book of theory or argument, but pure fiction. It's a joy to read, and I get to share that joy with everyone. There's no more delightful work than that, exhausting or not.

And so I've already started wanting to decide what the book for the third session will be. I've thought of some candidates, and I've begun reading a few of them. "Oh, this is lovely, this is wonderful," I think, feeling like a high school kid again. "Thank you so much for writing this," I find myself wanting to say, almost moved to express my gratitude. I'll definitely do the third session, so please look forward to it.

With all that in mind, and to prepare for any sudden karaoke, I just whipped up a batch of homemade throat remedy. I made it in a snap, poured it into a bottle, and stashed it in the fridge. All in all, I'd say my days aren't half bad.
