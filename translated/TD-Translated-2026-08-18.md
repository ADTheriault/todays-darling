## Title: Is a world full of optimal solutions really okay?
## Date: 2026-08-18
## Author: Shigesato Itoi
## Content:

When you're about to start something, when you first begin turning it over in your mind, even if you have "no idea where to begin," I think that's actually pretty normal. You fumble around, this isn't it, that isn't it, what do I do? Was there a hint somewhere? Now I've lost the thread entirely... That's the kind of thing everyone has always done.

If it's a problem with a clear answer, someone practiced at solving them can rattle it off, sure. But most of the things we mull over in this world have answers that are vague at best. More than that, often we can't even properly make out what the problem is.

And yet, these days, if you just ask an "AI," it'll whip up the "optimal solution" for you in no time.

Someone I know, a person quite at home in life abroad, apparently traveled to a foreign country for the first time over the summer holidays. Up until now, the way they'd do it was this: for that first night's restaurant, you'd ask the guidebook or the hotel and have a safe, reliable meal. Then the next day you'd wander through the streets and happen to duck into some little place where the locals were lining up. That was the sort of rhythm of it.

But now, apparently, you can't help asking the "AI." The moment you arrive, it'll tell you about the kind of place "where the locals line up," the sort of spot you'd normally only stumble onto by luck. Delicious, pleasant, a flawless "optimal solution."

And this is wonderful, of course it is, it was a good thing. But still, they said, a thought crossed their mind.

If you keep chasing after these "optimal solutions" that the "AI" hands you, one after another, aren't you turning into "a person who's just following instructions"? A human being becomes an "automatic device" that "processes" "optimal solutions." Honestly, I get the feeling we're already halfway there.

Isn't there supposed to be more to living than that? Even if the "optimal solution" really is the optimal answer.
